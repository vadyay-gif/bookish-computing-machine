A few useful answers

Before you start.

Do I need to record everything in my kitchen?

No. Start with a few ingredients you want to use. You can build up your kitchen inventory over time.

Can I change a meal plan?

Yes. The plan is a starting point you can review and adjust. Bonmeal is designed to keep the kitchen, meals and shopping connected as your plans change.

What about allergies and dietary restrictions?

They belong in your household’s planning preferences. Always review recipe ingredients and product labels yourself. Recipe suggestions and image recognition cannot guarantee that a meal is safe for an allergy.

How much will Bonmeal cost?

Monthly and yearly subscriptions are planned. Current prices, available offers and eligibility will be shown in the app before you subscribe.

Is this a web version of the app?

This website introduces Bonmeal. The interactive example uses sample ingredients; it doesn’t create or change a real meal plan.
